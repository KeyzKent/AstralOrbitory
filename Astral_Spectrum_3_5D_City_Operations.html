FLIGHT + BUILD OPERATIONS

W / S fly forwards / backwards; A / D strafe; Q / E go down/up; Shift boosts your speed. Drag the scene to change direction. Mouse wheel moves along your view direction.

Use ADVANCE DISTRICT for a guided route through the spaceport, architecture district, refinery, orbital dock, and motor underdeck. Click any building or use MODULE ARCHITECT to select, swap, scale, rotate, and move it. Export your version in STL or JSON.

Open FLUX LAB for simulated motor power and magnetic direction. Open NETWORK I/O for Base64/Hex/Binary/URI/JSON conversion or an opt-in WebSocket link. REPAIR OPS tracks equipment work orders across electronics, spacecraft, transit, and buildings.

The browser animates ships and rotor equipment; STL is a static format and cannot store movement or interaction. The plasma / hyperspace / levitation features are speculative visualizations.
